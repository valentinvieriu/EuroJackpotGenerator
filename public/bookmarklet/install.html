Lotto Bayern number filler

This bookmarklet uses your Eurojackpot app’s server algorithms and fills the normal ticket form.

  1. Drag the link below onto your browser’s bookmarks bar.
  2. Open Lotto Bayern’s Eurojackpot Normalschein and click the bookmark.
  3. Paste numbers or expand Generate using the app’s algorithms. Preview, then click Fill ticket fields.
Lotto Bayern · Fill numbers

Four generation methods

  • Uniform: unbiased random selection.
  • Weighted: square-root historical frequencies, sampled without replacement.
  • Favourites: your app’s favourite weighting and statistics fallback.
  • Unpopular: the app’s candidate-ranking heuristic; not calibrated player popularity.

Generation uses /api/generate on the app URL entered in the panel. The updated server must be deployed to allow requests from Lotto Bayern. The bookmarklet sends no account cookies.

There is no forced odd/even or high/low distribution. Methods change number selection, not the probability that an individual combination is drawn.

Accepts arrays of app tickets with mainNumbers and euroNumbers, earlier mains/euros objects, and Lotto Bayern payloads. Only standard 5+2 lines fit this form. System tickets are rejected rather than truncated.

Imports replace consecutive fields. Undo restores the previous fill while the panel remains open. Review the numbers and price on Lotto Bayern; saving and playing remain manual.
